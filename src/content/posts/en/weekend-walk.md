## Leave some open time

There is no need to choose a destination or fill every hour. A bottle of water and comfortable shoes are enough to begin.

After a long time at a screen, it becomes natural to measure the day by completed tasks. A walk gives time a different shape: no delivery date and no required finish line.

## Notice the small things

Leaves have changed color a little. A bakery smells warm. Someone is reading on a bench. Ordinary details give the day texture again.

Try recording three things:

1. A color you saw.
2. A sound you heard.
3. A sentence that came to mind.

A few words are enough to keep the memory.

## Bring an idea home

Sometimes a difficult problem becomes clearer after leaving the computer. Thinking needs space, too.

Make yourself a favorite drink when you return. If you feel like writing, those few words can become a post.

## Allow yourself to slow down

A weekend can include real rest. Paying attention to the weather and enjoying some quiet time are worthwhile things to do.
