## Start with one corner

Begin with the part of the desk you use most. Put away unused cables and give your cup a regular place.

The aim is to make the next time you sit down feel a little easier.

## Keep the list short

Write the three things you most want to finish today. Be specific: “Read an article and take notes” gives you an action to begin.

For a large task, write down the first step. A step you can take immediately is useful direction.

## Clear the digital desk too

Browser tabs, downloads, and screenshots slowly take up attention. Archive completed work and keep understandable categories for things you may need later.

## Keep something you like

A postcard, a plant, or favorite keycaps can make this corner feel like yours. A comfortable workspace can be simple and should fit the way you actually live.
